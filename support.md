# Piano Player — Support

Piano Player (on the App Store: **Pianos Player**) helps you practise the piano along with a video: slow it down without changing the pitch, repeat a passage from A to B, and play imported videos in the tuning of your own piano.

## Contact

Questions, problems or ideas: **arexolde@gmail.com**

Please mention your device (for example iPhone 15, iPad Air, Pixel 8), its system version and what you did when the problem happened.

## Frequently asked questions

**A YouTube video does not play.**
Some videos do not allow playback in other apps; the app then offers to open the video in YouTube. Check the internet connection, too.

**Why can't I retune a YouTube video?**
YouTube's player does not allow changing the sound. Tuning correction works for imported and recorded videos.

**The tuning measurement does not finish.**
Put the device near the piano in a quiet room and play each note clearly, letting it ring for about a second.

**How do I get my purchase on a new device?**
Open Settings → Full version → Restore purchase. On iPhone, iPad and Mac use the same Apple Account; on Android, the same Google account.

**Where are my videos stored?**
Only on your device, inside the app. See the [privacy policy](./).

**How do I delete everything?**
Delete videos in the app, or remove the app from the device.
