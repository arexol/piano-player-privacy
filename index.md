# Piano Player — Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** October 14, 2026

This privacy policy explains how the Android app **Piano Player** (package `com.pianoplayer`, "the app") handles your information. The app is developed and published by **Oleksandr Serkin** ("the developer").

## Summary

- The app has **no accounts**, **no analytics**, **no advertising** and **no tracking**.
- Everything you create in the app stays **on your device**. The developer has no servers and receives none of your data.
- The microphone is used only during piano calibration, and the sound is analysed on the device. It is **never recorded, stored or sent anywhere**.
- Some features rely on Google services: YouTube for playing YouTube videos and Google Play for the purchase. Those services handle data under Google's own privacy policy.

## Information stored on your device

The app stores the following in its private storage on your device:

- Videos you import from your device or record with your camera. They are copied into the app's private storage.
- YouTube links you add to the app.
- Your practice settings for each video: repeat range points, playback speed and similar settings.
- Piano tuning profiles created during calibration. A profile holds the measured pitch of your piano, not any audio.
- App settings, such as the interface language.
- The start date of the free trial and whether you have bought the full version.

This information is not sent to the developer or to anyone else. It is not included in Android cloud backups. It is deleted when you delete it in the app, clear the app's data, or uninstall the app.

## Permissions

| Permission | Why it is used |
|---|---|
| Microphone (`RECORD_AUDIO`) | Only while you calibrate the app to your piano. The sound is processed in real time to find the pitch of the notes you play. No audio is recorded, saved or sent. You can refuse this permission and use the rest of the app. |
| Internet and network state | To play YouTube videos and to talk to Google Play for the purchase. |
| Google Play billing (`BILLING`) | To offer the one-time purchase of the full version. |

The app does not use the camera directly. When you choose to record a video, the app opens your device's camera app. The resulting video is saved only in Piano Player's private storage.

## Third-party services

### YouTube

YouTube videos play in the official embedded YouTube player, which is provided by Google. While a YouTube video plays, Google may collect information such as your IP address, device information and viewing activity, as described in the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and the [YouTube Terms of Service](https://www.youtube.com/t/terms). The developer does not receive this information.

### Google Play billing

The purchase of the full version is processed entirely by Google Play. The app never sees your payment details. It receives only the information it needs to unlock the full version: whether the purchase is complete, pending or refunded. See the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and the [Google Play Terms of Service](https://play.google.com/about/play-terms/).

## Sharing

The app shares nothing by itself. When you tap **Share** on a video, the app opens the Android share menu, and the video or link goes only to the app or contact you choose.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13. It does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children, because it collects no personal information at all.

## Security

Your data stays in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read. Because nothing is sent to the developer, there is no copy of your data on any server.

## Your choices

- You can delete any video, together with its practice settings, inside the app.
- You can delete all app data in Android Settings → Apps → Piano Player → Storage → Clear storage, or by uninstalling the app.
- You can deny or revoke the microphone permission at any time in Android Settings.
