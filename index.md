# Piano Player — Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** October 23, 2026

This privacy policy explains how the app **Piano Player** ("the app") handles your information. It covers:

- the Android app (package `com.pianoplayer`), published on Google Play as **Piano Player**;
- the iPhone, iPad and Mac app (bundle ID `com.pianoplayer`), published on the App Store as **Pianos Player**.

The app is developed and published by **Oleksandr Serkin** ("the developer"). Where the platforms differ, this policy says so.

## Summary

- The app has **no accounts**, **no analytics**, **no advertising** and **no tracking**.
- Everything you create in the app stays **on your device**. The developer has no servers and receives none of your data.
- The microphone is used to measure the pitch of your piano (calibration and the tuner); that sound is analysed on the device and is **never recorded, stored or sent anywhere**. When you film a lesson with the camera, the video is recorded with its sound and kept only in the app.
- Some features rely on outside services: YouTube for playing YouTube videos, and Google Play or the App Store for the free trial and the purchase. Those services handle data under their own privacy policies.

## Information stored on your device

The app stores the following in its own storage on your device:

- Videos you import from your device or record with your camera. They are copied into the app's storage.
- YouTube links you add to the app.
- Your practice settings for each video: repeat range points, playback speed and similar settings.
- Piano tuning profiles created during calibration. A profile holds the measured pitch of your piano, not any audio.
- App settings, such as the interface language.
- The state of the free trial and whether you have bought the full version.

This information is not sent to the developer or to anyone else.

**Backups.**

- **Android:** the app's data is not included in Android cloud backups.
- **iPhone, iPad and Mac:** video files are excluded from iCloud, computer and Time Machine backups. The rest of the list above (links, practice settings, tuning profiles, settings and thumbnails) can be part of **your own** device backup, which is stored in your iCloud account or on your computer under Apple's terms; the developer has no access to it. The trial and purchase state is kept in the device's keychain, only on that device.

**Deleting.** The information is deleted when you delete it in the app, or when you clear the app's data or uninstall the app (see "Your choices"). On iPhone and iPad the small trial and purchase record in the keychain can remain after the app is removed; it contains no personal information, and the App Store restores your purchase from your Apple Account in any case.

## Permissions

| Permission | Platform | Why it is used |
|---|---|---|
| Microphone | all | While you calibrate the app to your piano or use the tuner, the sound is processed in real time to find the pitch of the notes you play; nothing of it is recorded, saved or sent. On iPhone and iPad it also records the sound of a lesson you film. You can refuse this permission and use the rest of the app. |
| Camera | iPhone, iPad | Only when you choose to record a lesson: the system camera screen opens and the video is saved only in the app's storage. The Mac app does not use the camera. |
| Internet and network state | all | To play YouTube videos and to talk to Google Play or the App Store for the trial and the purchase. |
| Google Play billing | Android | To offer the one-time purchase of the full version. |

On Android the app does not use the camera directly: when you choose to record a video, it opens your device's camera app, and the resulting video is saved only in Piano Player's storage.

## Third-party services

### YouTube

YouTube videos play in the official embedded YouTube player, which is provided by Google. While a YouTube video plays, Google may collect information such as your IP address, device information and viewing activity, as described in the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and the [YouTube Terms of Service](https://www.youtube.com/t/terms). The developer does not receive this information.

### Purchases: Google Play and the App Store

The free trial and the purchase of the full version are processed entirely by the store you got the app from:

- **Android:** Google Play — see the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and the [Google Play Terms of Service](https://play.google.com/about/play-terms/).
- **iPhone, iPad and Mac:** the App Store, with your Apple Account — see [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/) and the [Apple Media Services Terms](https://www.apple.com/legal/internet-services/itunes/).

The app never sees your payment details or your account. It receives only what it needs to unlock features: whether the trial has started and when, and whether the purchase is complete, pending or refunded.

## Sharing

The app shares nothing by itself. When you tap **Share** on a video, the app opens the system share menu, and the video or link goes only to the app or contact you choose.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13. It does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children, because it collects no personal information at all.

## Security

Your data stays in the app's own storage, which other apps cannot read. Because nothing is sent to the developer, there is no copy of your data on any server of the developer.

## Your choices

- You can delete any video, together with its practice settings, inside the app.
- **Android:** delete all app data in Settings → Apps → Piano Player → Storage → Clear storage, or uninstall the app.
- **iPhone and iPad:** delete the app (touch and hold its icon → Remove App → Delete App); this deletes all its data on the device.
- **Mac:** move the app to the Bin and delete its folder `~/Library/Containers/com.pianoplayer`.
- You can deny or revoke the microphone and camera permissions at any time in the system settings (Android: Settings → Apps → Piano Player → Permissions; Apple: Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone or Camera).
